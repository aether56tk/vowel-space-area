3. Quadrilateral vowel graph

F2 is horizontal and F1 is vertical. VSA uses only the four corner vowels /iː/, /æ/, /ɑː/ and /uː/. The polygon is formed in the fixed order /iː/ → /æ/ → /ɑː/ → /uː/. The other six vowels are shown only as additional acoustic points and are not included in VSA.
● Corner vowels /iː æ ɑː uː/ = VSA polygon● Other vowels = acoustic points

Results will appear here after the four corner vowels are entered.

5. Method

VSA: ½ × |Σ(F2ᵢ×F1ᵢ₊₁ − F1ᵢ×F2ᵢ₊₁)|, using only the four corner vowels /iː/ → /æ/ → /ɑː/ → /uː/. Area is reported in Hz². This tool is an analysis aid and should be checked against the approved research protocol before thesis/publication use.
