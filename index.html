Vowel Space Area Analyzer

Enter only F1 and F2. Everything else is calculated automatically. The four corner vowels are used for quadrilateral VSA; the remaining vowels are available for acoustic vowel-space plotting.

1. Paste your research data

Paste a spreadsheet row containing: Sl. no, Name, Gender, LEAP score, Proficiency, then the 10 vowel F1/F2/VD groups. The analyzer automatically ignores VD and uses only F1/F2.

1. Enter F1 and F2

No duration, trial number, participant details, or other measurements are required. F1 and F2 are in Hz. Enter the values you have; the four corner vowels must be present to calculate VSA.

2. Automatic VSA results

3. Quadrilateral vowel graph

The polygon is drawn automatically through /iː/ → /æ/ → /ɑː/ → /uː/. F2 is horizontal and F1 is vertical; the four corner vowels define the quadrilateral VSA.

5. Method used

Quadrilateral VSAOrdered points: /iː/ → /æ/ → /ɑː/ → /uː/. VSA is calculated with the shoelace equation and reported in Hz².
Additional vowels/ɪ, ɛ, ʌ, ʊ, ɔː, ɝ/ are plotted when F1/F2 are supplied, but they are not included in the four-corner VSA.
